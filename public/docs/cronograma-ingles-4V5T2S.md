# CRONOGRAMA 4V5T2S — 12 SEMANAS

---

## PÁGINA 1 — ROTINA DIÁRIA (copie na primeira folha)

**25–30 min por dia**

1. **5 min** — Ler a célula em voz alta: pergunta / afirmativa / negativa
2. **10 min** — Escrever 10 perguntas (cruzar a célula com os wh- da esquerda)
3. **5 min** — Responder em voz alta, sobre a MINHA vida real
4. **5 min** — Revisar a célula da semana passada, sem olhar o mapa

**Só avanço quando:** 15 perguntas em 2 min, em voz alta, sem olhar.
Pelo menos 5 na 3ª pessoa (he/she/it).

**Todo dia, 5 min antes de dormir:**
- 5 verbos irregulares (go-went-gone)
- 1 phrasal verb (look at / look for / look after)
- Pronomes: I – my – me

## PÁGINA 2 — AS 12 SEMANAS

| Sem | O que estudar |
|-----|---------------|
| 1  | PRESENTE – linha A → do you / does he |
| 2  | PRESENTE – linha B → are you / is he + V-ING |
| 3  | PRESENTE – linhas B2 e C → is there any / can-must-should |
| 4  | PASSADO – linha A → did you + pronúncia do -ED |
| 5  | PASSADO – linhas B, B2, C → was-were / was there / could |
| 6  | **REVISÃO** presente x passado embaralhado |
| 7  | FUTURO – todas as linhas → will |
| 8  | B3 – os três "vou": going to / every / think you will |
| 9  | PERFECT – have you + v3 / yet / already |
| 10 | PERFECT – have you EVER / been to / never |
| 11 | HOW LONG – for x since |
| 12 | WOULD (V+RIA) – todas as linhas |

**Encaixar nas semanas 6 e 12:** comparativo/superlativo, may-might-could, some/every.

## PÁGINA 3 — EXEMPLO DE UM DIA COMPLETO
### (Semana 1 — célula PRESENTE / linha A)

**Passo 1 — ler em voz alta (5 min)**

- Pergunta: Do you work here? / Does he work here?
- Afirmativa: I work here. / He work**s** here.
- Negativa: I don't work here. / He doesn't work here.

**Passo 2 — escrever 10 perguntas (10 min)**

Pego a célula `do you / does he` e passo pela coluna dos wh-:

1. What do you do?
2. Where do you work?
3. When do you start?
4. Why does he work so hard?
5. How often do you study?
6. What time does she wake up?
7. What kind of music do you listen to?
8. How much does it cost?
9. Who studies here?  ← *sem do!*
10. How far do you live from here?

**Passo 3 — responder sobre a minha vida (5 min)**

- I work at a school. I start at 8. My brother works a lot because he needs money.
- She wakes up at 6. I listen to rock. It costs 20 reais.

**Passo 4 — revisar a semana anterior (5 min)**
*(na semana 1 não tem — usar pra repetir o passo 2)*

**Caça-erro do dia:** he go**s** ❌ → does he **go** ✅

## PÁGINA 7 — PROMPTS: PERFECT (semanas 9–10)

**Fórmula:** HAVE + V3
**Marcadores (sem tempo definido):** recently – today – this morning – all my life – once – many times

```
1.  Have you _______ (v3) today?
2.  Has he _______ (v3) this morning?
3.  Have you _______ (v3) YET?
4.  I've JUST _______ (v3).
5.  I've ALREADY _______ (v3).
6.  No, I haven't _______ (v3) yet.
7.  Have you EVER _______ (v3)?
8.  Have you ever been to _______ ?
9.  I have NEVER _______ (v3) before.
10. How many times have you _______ (v3)?
```

**Traduções-chave:** JÁ = yet (negativa) · JÁ alguma vez = ever

---

## PÁGINA 8 — PROMPTS: HOW LONG (semana 11)

```
1. How long have you been _______ ing?
   → I have been _______ ing since _______ .

2. How long have you known _______ ?
   → I have known him FOR _______ years.

3. How long have you lived here?
   → I have lived here since 2004.
```

**FOR** = duração (for 5 years) · **SINCE** = ponto de partida (since 2004)
Verbos de estado (know, have, be, like) **não usam -ing**

## PÁGINA 9 — PROMPTS: WOULD / V+RIA (semana 12)

**Gatilho:** if you had… / if you were…

```
1. What would you _______ ?

5. Could you _______ if _______ ?
6. If I had money, I would _______ .
7. If I were you, I would _______ .
8. What would he do if _______ ?
```

## PÁGINA 10 — BLOCOS SOLTOS (encaixar nas revisões)

**Possibilidade:** may · might · could (+) / can (–)

**Comparativo e superlativo**

| | curto | longo |
|---|---|---|
| comparativo | tall**er** than | **more** difficult than |
| superlativo | **the** tall**est** | **the most** difficult |

**Verbos com TO:** have · plan · need · like · use · want **to** + verbo

**Partículas:** in-out · on-off · up-down · away/off-back · over-around · to-for · in-on-at · into · through · about
